Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

AutoUploader_bam ("the Application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you connect your TikTok account to the Application.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the Application through TikTok Login Kit, we request access only to the following scopes:

  • user.info.basic: Basic profile information (such as your Open ID, display name, and avatar) used solely to identify the connected account in the interface.
  • video.publish: Permission to upload and publish video content that you explicitly select and confirm.

2. How We Use Information

Your information and OAuth tokens are used strictly to authenticate your session, retrieve your creator publishing settings, and upload videos you choose to publish. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and video files are stored locally on the user's own device. The Application does not operate external servers that retain copies of your videos or personal data.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You may revoke the Application's access to your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings & Privacy → Security & Permissions → Apps and Services). You may also delete your local configuration file (tiktok_config.json) at any time to permanently remove all stored credentials.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or data deletion requests, contact us at tu-correo@ejemplo.com.
