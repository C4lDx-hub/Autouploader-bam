AutoUploader_bam

A personal content management and video publishing tool that allows creators to prepare metadata and publish original short-form videos directly to their authorized social media accounts, including TikTok.

Key Features

  • Secure account authentication via official TikTok OAuth 2.0 (Login Kit).
  • Review and edit video captions, privacy settings, and interaction controls before publishing.
  • Direct video publishing to authorized TikTok accounts using the official Content Posting API.
